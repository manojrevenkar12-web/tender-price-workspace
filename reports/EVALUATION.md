# Evaluation

Generated by `pnpm --filter @tpw/sim evaluate` on the seeded database (1,979,829 historical unit prices, 31,697 distinct position wordings). Do not edit by hand.

Every case asks for the market range of one kind of work, with one combination of its attributes, in one of BE, BY, SN, NW, for 2026Q4 (the quarter after the history ends), and compares the answer with the simulation's true market price. Two wording sets:

- **dev**: the catalog's development wordings, used while the matcher was built.
- **regression v1**: the first blind set. Its failures (soil classes written "BK 3-5", thicknesses as "Wanddicke 30 cm", cable sections in "qmm", abbreviations such as Hlz, ZE, WT, UV, Cu, AIV) were studied and fixed, so it is now a regression set; its first run is kept in `reports/regression/blind-v1/FIRST_RUN.json`.
- **blind (v2)**: 4 new wordings per kind of work, written from `packages/sim/blind/BRIEF.md` alone by an author who saw neither the history wordings, the matcher, nor the first set, after those fixes. Its first run (2026-10-26) is kept in `reports/blind/FIRST_RUN.json` and is never rewritten; the matcher was not changed against it.
