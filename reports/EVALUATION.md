# Evaluation

Generated by `pnpm --filter @tpw/sim evaluate` on the seeded database (1,979,829 historical unit prices, 31,697 distinct position wordings). Do not edit by hand.

Every case asks for the market range of one kind of work, with one combination of its attributes, in one of BE, BY, SN, NW, for 2026Q4 (the quarter after the history ends), and compares the answer with the simulation's true market price. Two wording sets:

- **dev**: the catalog's development wordings, used while the matcher was built.
- **blind**: 4 wordings per kind of work written from `packages/sim/blind/BRIEF.md` alone, by an author who saw neither the history wordings nor the matcher. Its first run (2026-10-26) is kept in `reports/blind/FIRST_RUN.json` and is never rewritten; the matcher was not changed against it.

Coverage is the share of cases that got a published range; the rest were suppressed (dev: {"no_data":4}, blind: {"no_data":184,"too_few_points":4}). "Same work" is the share of the matching weight that fell on historical wordings of the same kind of work, from the simulation's truth, which the platform never reads. No published range rests on fewer than five companies (disclosure control).
